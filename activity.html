How to read these numbers

The calendar shows the past 365 days. The statistics above cover the 30 completed calendar days before today (using UTC to determine today). Daily dates and token counts are kept exactly as returned by Codex; today may not be reported yet.

Unreported dates have a striped square, distinct from a reported zero. Totals and active days include only reported dates, and the daily average is divided by the number of reported dates. Older daily records are retained between syncs.

Green levels are fixed: below 1 million, 1–10 million, 10–100 million, and 100 million or more tokens. These are usage counts, not working hours, spending, or a measure of productivity. The source does not provide an input/output breakdown here.
